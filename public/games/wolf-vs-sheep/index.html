How to Play

  • Sheep move one step to an adjacent empty node.
  • Wolf moves one step to an adjacent empty node.
  • Wolf can jump over adjacent sheep to capture.
  • Sheep win by trapping the wolf.
  • Wolf wins by capturing 7 sheep.
